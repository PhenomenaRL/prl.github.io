Our Work

At PRL, we are redefining autonomy by bridging the gap between engineered efficiency and biological resilience. Modern systems are built for performance, but they lack the homeostatic ability to self-repair and endure without human intervention. We don't just build machines; we develop robotic systems designed to endure, forever, on Earth & beyond.
