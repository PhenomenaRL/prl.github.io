High-Fidelity Fluid Dynamics in Neural Radiance Fields

Jane Doe, John Smith, and the PRL Team

Abstract: We present a novel framework for integrating differentiable fluid dynamics directly into the optimization loop of Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF). By leveraging particle-based simulations constrained by volumetric density gradients, we achieve real-time replication of complex fluid phenomena with unprecedented visual fidelity.

Introduction

Replicating fluid motion has long been a challenge in computer vision and graphics. Traditional methods rely on heavy simulation or heuristic-based rendering. In this work, we bridge the gap between physical accuracy and neural representation by introducing a physics-informed loss function that operates on NeRF volumes.

Methodology

Our approach involves three key components:

  1. Differentiable Lagrangian Solver: A custom particle-based engine that allows gradients to flow from the pixel loss back to the fluid state.
  2. Volume-Aware Density Mapping: A technique to translate sparse particle data into continuous density fields compatible with volumetric rendering.
  3. Temporal Consistency Regularization: Ensuring that the generated frames maintain physical coherence across long sequences.

Results

Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art volumetric rendering techniques in both visual quality (PSNR/SSIM) and physical plausibility. We show successful replications of smoke, water, and non-Newtonian fluids.
